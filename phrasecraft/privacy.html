Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Effective: October 14, 2026 / 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日

Phrasecraft Piano does not collect or upload your personal data.

Local processing

The app requires no account and contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. MIDI files selected by you, MIDI keyboard events, recordings, lesson progress, phrase reviews, and settings are processed and stored on your Mac. No MIDI files or performance data are sent to a server.

Files and system capabilities

Import uses the macOS file picker. Export writes WAV, M4A, MIDI, or practice-report text files only to a location you choose. The app uses audio output, Core MIDI, and the macOS system sound bank. Internal audio recording does not access your microphone. It does not access your camera, contacts, location, or photos.

Deletion

Delete individual Takes in the app. Exported files can be deleted in Finder. Local app data is stored in the app sandbox; removing the app alone may leave saved data on your Mac. Contact support for help removing local settings and practice history.

When you contact support

Information you choose to send by email is used to respond to your request. Do not send private MIDI files or recordings unless you want them reviewed for your support request.
