Phrasecraft Piano is a native Mac workspace for practicing phrases from your own recordings and MIDI files.

Import or record → split into phrases → practice with pitch guidance → review each note → revisit at a slower speed.

A practice loop built around your material

  • Import standard MIDI format 0/1 locally, or record your own Take.
  • Group simultaneous notes into chord targets and divide them into 4, 8, or 16-target phrases.
  • Practice all registers, notes below C4, or notes from C4 upward.
  • See mistakes and signed onset offsets in milliseconds for each target.
  • Keep the latest 30 completed phrase reviews locally and export a text report.

Includes an interactive built-in phrase example, 12 foundation lessons, 30 beginner pieces, an 88-key piano, MIDI keyboard input, recording, Piano Roll editing, loops, and audio/MIDI export. No account, ads, or analytics.

乐句工坊

把自己的录音和 MIDI 变成分段练习。按 4、8 或 16 个目标音拆分乐句，选择音域，跟随五线谱和绿色琴键练习，查看每个位置的错音与落键时机偏差，降速再练。最近 30 次完成的乐句复盘保存在本机，也可导出文本报告。

首次打开就能用内置示例完整体验，无需外接设备。支持 macOS 14 及以上。
