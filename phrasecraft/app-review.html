Phrasecraft Piano review walkthrough

Version 1.0, build 3. Formerly Concert Piano. The new home is Phrase Studio.

  1. Launch the app, which opens Phrase Studio with a built-in 16-target example split into two phrases.
  2. Select phrase 1 and Start Practice. Wait for the four count-in beats. Play the green notes using the mouse or Mac keyboard: C4, E4, G4, C5, B4, G4, E4, D4 (physical keys A, D, G, K, J, G, D, S).
  3. Intentionally play a wrong note to see a red mistake marker. Continue playing the correct targets. Missed positions are automatically reviewed after the first pass.
  4. Open Practice Review after completion. View per-target mistakes and signed timing offsets in milliseconds. Export Review produces a plain-text report. Revisit This Phrase at 75% Speed returns to the exercise at a slower speed.
  5. Choose 4 or 16 targets per phrase to see the phrase map change. Choose the lower register to verify filtering; this treble-only example has no lower notes. The pitch split is C4.
  6. Record your own notes as a Take and choose the Take in Phrase Studio, or use Import MIDI with a standard format 0/1 MIDI file. The same practice workflow is generated from user material. Import is local and does not modify the original file.

No login, internet, purchase, or external MIDI device is required. The built-in example is an actual interactive practice source, with no pre-populated scores.

Scope of timing feedback

Scores evaluate note onset timing relative to the chosen phrase speed. They do not evaluate note release, real-world acoustic piano audio, or hand/finger technique. Register filtering does not infer left/right hand parts.

Other available workflows: 12 foundation lessons, 30 beginner pieces, 88-key mouse/keyboard playing, Core MIDI input, local recording, editable Piano Roll, A/B loops, and WAV/M4A/MIDI export.
