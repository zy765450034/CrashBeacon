Support / 技术支持

Start with Phrase Studio

  1. Open the app. Phrase Studio is the default home.
  2. Use the built-in example, click Import MIDI, or record a Take and select it in the source menu.
  3. Choose a register and targets per phrase, then choose a numbered phrase in Phrase Map.
  4. Click Start Practice, wait through the four count-in beats, and play the green targets.
  5. After finishing and reviewing missed targets, open Practice Review. Revisit at 75% speed or export the report.

Keyboard and sound

A–; play notes, Z/X change octaves, C/V change velocity, and Space controls sustain. You may also click the keys or connect a Core MIDI keyboard. If there is no sound, check system output and app volume, then select Stop All.

MIDI import

Use standard MIDI format 0 or 1, under 10 MB and fewer than 50,000 notes. Pitched notes outside A0–C8 and MIDI percussion channel 10 are excluded. Original MIDI files are never modified. Register filtering is a pitch split at C4, not automatic identification of left and right hands. Timing reviews measure note onset, not note release or fingering.

Recordings and data

Recordings and imported MIDI Takes appear in the Take menu. Select and delete a Take to remove it. Practice reviews are stored locally. Exported audio remains the original recording when Piano Roll notes are edited; playback and MIDI export follow the edited notes.
